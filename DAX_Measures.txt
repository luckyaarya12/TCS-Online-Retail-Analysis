CORE FINANCIAL MEASURES (DAX):-
 
REVENUE MEASURE
   Rationale: Calculates total sales revenue based on data that has been pre-cleaned in Power Query 
   (Quantity > 0 and Cancelled Orders removed), ensuring high integrity for executive reports.
   This measure is used to drive the Monthly Revenue Trends visual on the dashboard.
    
Revenue = SUMX('Online Retail', 'Online Retail'[Quantity] * 'Online Retail'[UnitPrice])

TOTAL CLEANED ORDERS
   Rationale: Counts all rows in the cleaned 'Online Retail' table to represent total valid transactions.
   Total Cleaned Orders = COUNTROWS('Online Retail')
